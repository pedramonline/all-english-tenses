01

Explanation

Use the Present Simple for routines, facts, repeated actions, states, and things you see as generally true. It is the tense of your normal life, not necessarily what is happening this second.

PastNowFuture

Repeated or generally true around the present. There is usually no important start or finish point.

YOU = singular or plural. HE / SHE / IT often form an important grammar group.

03

When do we use it?

  • habits and routines: things you do regularly
  • facts and general truths
  • states, feelings, opinions, possession
  • fixed timetables and schedules
  • frequency: always, often, sometimes, rarely, never

04

Sentence formation

English examples stay in English.

+ Affirmative
Formula: Subject + base verb (he/she/it: verb + s/es)
  • Luca plays basketball after school.Luca spelar basket efter skolan.
  • Nora knows the answer.Nora vet svaret.
  • The library is quiet after lunch.Biblioteket är tyst efter lunch.
− Negative
Formula: Subject + do/does not + base verb | be: am/is/are + not
  • We don’t stream videos during class.Vi streamar inte videor under lektionen.
  • Amir doesn’t own a car.Amir äger ingen bil.
  • I am not tired today.Jag är inte trött idag.
? (Y/N) Yes/No question
Formula: Do/Does + subject + base verb? | Am/Is/Are + subject ...?
  • Does Ella practice the guitar every day?Övar Ella gitarr varje dag?
  • Do you prefer tea or coffee?Föredrar du te eller kaffe?
  • Are your parents at home?Är dina föräldrar hemma?
? (Wh) Wh- question
Formula: Wh-word + do/does + subject + base verb? | Wh-word + be + subject ...?
  • Where does Leo catch the bus?Var tar Leo bussen?
  • Why do they believe that rumor?Varför tror de på det ryktet?
  • How are you today?Hur mår du idag?
−? (Y/N) Negative Yes/No question
Formula: Don’t/Doesn’t + subject + base verb? | Isn’t/Aren’t + subject ...?
  • Doesn’t Maya take the tram to school?Tar inte Maya spårvagnen till skolan?
  • Don’t your friends know about the test?Vet inte dina vänner om provet?
  • Isn’t the cafeteria open today?Är inte cafeterian öppen idag?
−? (Wh) Negative Wh- question
Formula: Wh-word + don’t/doesn’t + subject + base verb? | Wh-word + isn’t/aren’t + subject ...?
  • Why doesn’t Noah answer the group chat?Varför svarar inte Noah i gruppchatten?
  • Why don’t they bring lunch from home?Varför tar de inte med lunch hemifrån?
  • Why isn’t Leo at football practice?Varför är inte Leo på fotbollsträningen?
? (Y/N)Question + answer models

Does Ella practice the guitar every day?

Positive answerYes, she does. She practices after dinner.
Negative answerNo, she doesn’t. She practices only on weekends.
? (Wh)Example answer

Where does Leo catch the bus?

He catches it at the stop outside school.

A Wh-question asks for specific information. The highlighted part is the new information that answers the question.

Negative questions

With a negative Yes/No question, “Yes” confirms the positive idea; “No” confirms the negative idea.

05

Real-life examples

  1. I check the class chat before school.Jag kollar klasschatten före skolan.
  2. My best friend lives two streets away.Min bästa vän bor två gator bort.
  3. The first lesson starts at 08:30.Den första lektionen börjar 08.30.
  4. I prefer studying with music.Jag föredrar att plugga med musik.
  5. Our gym is closed on Sundays.Vårt gym är stängt på söndagar.
Real-life scenario

He lives near school, takes the tram every morning, and usually meets his friends by the lockers. He loves music, but he does not play songs during lessons. On Fridays, his football practice starts at four. When someone asks, “What do you do after school?”, this is the tense Alex naturally uses to describe his regular life.

06

Common mistakes

  • For he/she/it, add -s/-es in affirmative sentences: She works, not She work.För he/she/it lägger du till -s/-es i jakande meningar: She works, inte She work.
  • After does/doesn’t, return to the base verb: Does he play? not Does he plays?Efter does/doesn’t använder du grundformen: Does he play? inte Does he plays?
  • Many stative verbs normally use Present Simple: I understand, I know, I want.Många tillståndsverb används normalt i Present Simple: I understand, I know, I want.

Memory tip

Ask yourself: “Is this generally true or part of a routine?” If yes, Present Simple is a strong candidate.

Real-life examples

  1. I am waiting for the tram right now.Jag väntar på spårvagnen just nu.

  3. More students are using AI study tools.Fler elever använder AI-verktyg för studier.
  4. She is always borrowing my charger!Hon lånar alltid min laddare!

Real-life scenario

It is lunch break. Lina is sitting with two friends, someone is showing a meme, and the others are laughing. A classmate is studying for a test because he is taking it after lunch. Outside, it is raining, so nobody is playing football. Lina is also meeting her cousin after school. Everything feels “in progress” or temporarily arranged, which is why Present Continuous fits.

06

Common mistakes

  • Do not normally use stative verbs in continuous forms: say I know, not I am knowing.Använd normalt inte tillståndsverb i continuous-former: säg I know, inte I am knowing.
  • Do not forget the verb be: She is studying, not She studying.Glöm inte verbet be: She is studying, inte She studying.
  • Use Present Simple for permanent/general situations and Present Continuous for temporary/current ones.Använd Present Simple för permanenta/allmänna situationer och Present Continuous för tillfälliga/pågående.

Memory tip

Imagine a phone camera recording the situation. If you can “see the action happening”, this tense often works.

01

Explanation

The Present Perfect connects the past to now. The action happened before now, but the exact finished time is not the focus. The result, experience, or unfinished time period matters now.

PastNowFuture

Something happened before NOW, but the result, experience, or unfinished time period connects it to NOW.

03

When do we use it?

  • past actions with a present result
  • life experiences when the exact time is not stated
  • unfinished time periods: today, this week, this year
  • states/actions from the past continuing until now
  • recent events with just, already, yet

Common time clues

alreadyjustyeteverneversinceforso farrecentlythis week
04

Sentence formation

English examples stay in English.

+ Affirmative
Formula: Subject + have/has + past participle (V3)
  • I have finished my project.Jag har gjort klart mitt projekt.
  • Zoe has known her best friend since year 7.Zoe har känt sin bästa vän sedan årskurs 7.
  • Our class has been quiet all morning.Vår klass har varit tyst hela morgonen.
− Negative
Formula: Subject + have/has not + past participle
  • They haven’t seen the new Marvel film.De har inte sett den nya Marvel-filmen.
  • He hasn’t owned a bike before.Han har inte ägt en cykel tidigare.
  • I haven’t been to Malmö yet.Jag har inte varit i Malmö än.
? (Y/N) Yes/No question
Formula: Have/Has + subject + past participle?
  • Have you tried Korean food?Har du provat koreansk mat?
  • Has Sara forgotten her password?Har Sara glömt sitt lösenord?
  • Have your parents been busy lately?Har dina föräldrar varit upptagna på sistone?
? (Wh) Wh- question
Formula: Wh-word + have/has + subject + past participle?
  • How long have you lived in Gothenburg?Hur länge har du bott i Göteborg?
  • Why has Max deleted the message?Varför har Max raderat meddelandet?
  • Where have they been since lunch?Var har de varit sedan lunch?
−? (Y/N) Negative Yes/No question
Formula: Haven’t/Hasn’t + subject + past participle?
  • Hasn’t Maya finished the presentation yet?Har inte Maya gjort klart presentationen än?
  • Haven’t you seen that video before?Har du inte sett den videon förut?
  • Haven’t they chosen a team name yet?Har de inte valt ett lagnamn än?
−? (Wh) Negative Wh- question
Formula: Wh-word + haven’t/hasn’t + subject + past participle?
  • Why hasn’t Noah replied yet?Varför har inte Noah svarat än?
  • Why haven’t you uploaded the file?Varför har du inte laddat upp filen?
  • Why haven’t they started the match?Varför har de inte startat matchen?

Negative answerNo, she hasn’t. She has only written the introduction.

05

Real-life examples

  1. I have finished my assignment, so I can relax.Jag har gjort klart min uppgift, så jag kan slappna av.
  2. Have you ever travelled alone?Har du någonsin rest ensam?
  3. We have had three tests this week.Vi har haft tre prov den här veckan.
  4. She has lived here since 2022.Hon har bott här sedan 2022.
  5. The teacher has just posted the grades.Läraren har precis lagt upp betygen.
Real-life scenario

Nico is getting ready for a school trip. He has packed his bag, has charged his phone, and has already downloaded the tickets. He has never visited Copenhagen, so he is excited. His friend has known the city for years and has promised to help. The important point is not exactly when Nico packed each item. What matters is the situation now: he is ready.

06

Common mistakes

  • Do not use a finished past time such as yesterday with Present Perfect: say I saw it yesterday.Använd inte en avslutad dåtid som yesterday med Present Perfect: säg I saw it yesterday.
  • Use the past participle, not always the past form: I have seen, not I have saw.Använd past participle, inte alltid preteritumformen: I have seen, inte I have saw.
  • Positive short answers are not contracted: Yes, I have.Jakande kortsvar dras inte ihop: Yes, I have.

Memory tip

Ask: “Does this past event still matter now, or is the time unfinished?” If yes, consider Present Perfect.

Use the Present Perfect Continuous for an activity that started in the past and has continued until now, or has just stopped with a visible result. It emphasizes activity and duration.

If your natural follow-up question is “How long?”, this tense is often a good fit.
